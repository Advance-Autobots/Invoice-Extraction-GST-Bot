WHAT THIS TOOL DOES:
This automation tool was built specifically for Chartered Accountants, tax 
practitioners, and audit teams to automate purchase invoice processing:
- Extracts supplier details, GSTINs, invoice numbers, dates, HSN codes, and tax figures.
- Validates statutory GST compliance under the CGST Act (intra vs. inter-state heads).
- Performs arithmetic cross-checks (Taxable Value + Taxes = Grand Total).
- Detects duplicate billing across batches (SHA-256 hash + invoice particulars).
- Automatically produces 3 professional audit Excel registers in 'output/'.
- Safely categorizes files into 'processed/' (clean) and 'exceptions/' (review needed).

HOW TO USE:
1. Place invoice files into: input/invoices/
2. Double-click: Invoice_Extractor.bat
3. View results in: output/Invoice_Register.xlsx & Exception_Report.xlsx

NEED SAMPLE BILLS TO TEST?
Run 'python create_sample_invoices.py' to generate 8 realistic test invoices.
